FAQ

Questions people ask first.

Simple answers before you reach out.

Can MendMode help remotely?

Yes. Most software, setup, account, browser, and workflow issues can be handled remotely through chat, screenshots, or guided steps.

How fast do you reply?

Most messages get a response the same day. Send the issue through Contact and include the app, device, and what already happened.

What if the issue cannot be fixed?

You still get a diagnosis, the likely cause, and the best next option, such as replacing a tool, contacting a provider, or changing setup.

Do I need to understand tech first?

No. Explain the problem in normal words. MendMode turns the messy details into practical steps.

Can you help with phones and laptops?

Yes. MendMode can help with common phone, laptop, browser, app, account, and setup problems.

Can you set up Windows or Linux?

Yes. MendMode can help with Windows and Linux setup, updates, drivers, essential apps, user accounts, and basic preferences.

Can you create a website?

Yes. MendMode can build simple websites, landing pages, service pages, contact flows, and clean public profiles.

Will my email or WhatsApp number show on the site?

No. Visitors only see Contact buttons first. The Email and WhatsApp options appear inside the contact popup.

Can every issue be fixed?

Not always. Some account, device, software, or third-party platform issues may depend on outside providers and cannot always be fully recovered or fixed.
